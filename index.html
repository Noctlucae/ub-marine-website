关于我们

Your local switchboard team.

本地团队，扎实做好每一台配电盘。

U B Marine Electrical Sdn. Bhd. is a Sarawak-based main switchboard (MSB) manufacturer and electrical contractor, serving marine, industrial and commercial projects alike. Our team handles the full workflow in-house — from AutoCAD circuit design and CNC sheet-metal cutting to panel wiring, testing and on-site commissioning.

U B Marine Electrical Sdn. Bhd. 总部位于马来西亚砂拉越，是一家主配电盘（MSB）制造商及电气承包商，服务范围涵盖船舶、工业及商用项目。我们的团队全程自主完成从AutoCAD电路设计、CNC钣金切割、盘柜布线、测试到现场调试的整套工作流程。

  • In-house AutoCAD circuit design自主AutoCAD电路设计
  • CNC metal sheet cutting & fabricationCNC钣金切割与制造
  • Certified up to 3000A MSB systems认证承接高达3000A的MSB系统
  • Marine-standard wiring & installation符合船级标准的布线与安装
